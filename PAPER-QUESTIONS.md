# Questions remaining after reading

The last section of each paper reader records explicit questions the user still cannot answer or understand. It can also collect unanswered reading-question items, with stable source IDs to prevent repeated imports. An empty reading answer is never assumed to be resolved. Question records are independent of reading-checklist completion and do not mark papers read or unread.

The separate paper-questions.html page groups questions by paper, displays original record dates and resolution dates, filters by date/status/text, and retains answers when resolved. A resolved question is crossed out and can be reopened; original record dates remain unchanged. The page entrance is near the top of Required Reading. Daily Effort shows daily paper questions immediately before conference DDL, including earlier unresolved questions and those resolved on the selected date. Scope counts and lifetime counts are shown separately.

Records live as paper-question entries in the ignored local study database. Updates check revision and paper ownership. Draft text is kept in the browser until saved; an edit made during a pending save is retained as a draft. There is no hard-delete interface. Tests cover dates, unresolved carry, resolution/reopening, duplicate import, revision/ownership validation and independence from reading logs. Isolated browser QA verified adding/importing questions, saving an answer, crossing out a resolved question, counts and persistence after reload. Production records were not populated with demonstration questions.
